Ordering Notes
==============

Each individual breakout board is 30x50mm. Estimated cost is about $155 for 5 boards including assembly and parts ($52 for just parts -> $10 per board).

# Basics
- Base Material: MFR-4
- Layers: 2

# PCB Specifications
- Deburring/Edge Routing: Yes
- PCB Thickness: 1.6mm is standard
- Surface Finish: OSP or Lead-Free HASL

# High-Spec Options
- Via Covering: Tented
- Min via hole size/diameter: 0.3mm


PCB Assembly
============

- PCBA Type: Economy (although Standard may be slightly cheaper because of the use of OSP)
- Every part needs to be checked for orientation, especially all ICs (aligning the dot with the arrow), diodes (align + sign), and connectors.

Parts Notes
===========

- Most expensive parts:
  - TPS62823DLCR:    $2.7949 (buck converter)
  - BQ25895RTWR:     $1.7215 (charger)
  - STC3115AIQT:     $1.4102 (fuel gauge; replaced BQ27441)
  - and then the connectors: ~$1.75 for the ones that will gone in the next version
